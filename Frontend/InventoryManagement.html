StockWise

Inventory management that keeps your business organized

StockWise is a full-stack web application designed to help small businesses manage products, monitor stock levels, and record inventory movements.

The problem

Small businesses may rely on manual records or spreadsheets to track their inventory. StockWise aims to make inventory management more organized and efficient.

What StockWise does

Manage products

Add products with a name, quantity and price, and see them stored by the backend.

Monitor stock levels

See at a glance what is in stock, running low or out of stock.

Record inventory movements

Keep track of stock coming in and going out as your inventory changes.

Who it’s for

Small business owners

Get a clear picture of your stock and what it’s worth.

Shop employees

Check quantities and update items quickly during the day.

Inventory administrators

Keep product records accurate and well organized.
